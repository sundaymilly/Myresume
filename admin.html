How it works

Everything you add or edit here is stored in the site's database (data/portfolio.db) and shown to visitors straight away. There is no publishing step.
  • Allowed files: PDF, Word (.doc/.docx), text and images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP), up to 15 MB each.
  • Backup: copy data/portfolio.db. It holds all text and documents.
  • Password: set with the ADMIN_PASSWORD setting on your host. Sessions last 12 hours.
