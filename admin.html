How publishing works

Important: your edits are saved in this browser and show on your portfolio when you open it in the same browser (a yellow “Previewing admin changes” tag appears). Recruiters only see what is on your web host.
  1. Make your changes here (documents and write-ups).
  2. Click Publish package. A file called portfolio-update.zip downloads.
  3. Unzip it and upload content.json and the documents folder to the same folder as portfolio.html on your host (replace the old ones).
  4. Refresh your live site. Recruiters now see the new documents and text.
Keep admin.html off your public site (or protect it with your host’s password feature). It has no login of its own, but it cannot change what recruiters see without the upload step above.
